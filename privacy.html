Privacy Policy

Cosmic Math (cosmicmath.in) lets you keep kundli charts for yourself and your family and ask questions about them. This page explains what we collect and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address, only to sign you in and to keep your data separate from everyone else's.
  • The details you enter for each kundli: name, date, time and place of birth.
  • The questions you ask and the answers given.

How it is used

Your data is used only to calculate charts and to answer your questions. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or show it to other users. Questions and chart details are sent to an AI service solely to produce the answer you asked for.

Storage and deletion

Your data is stored securely and tied to your Google email. You can delete any kundli from within the app. To have all your data removed, write to the support email below.

Google sign-in

We use Google Sign-In and request only your basic profile (name and email). We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: please write to the support email shown on the sign-in screen.
